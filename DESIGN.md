# Design System: Site Factory Skeleton

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The skeleton is not the look."**

The template owns structure only: which sections exist, in what order, how the name fills a phone, where the price column sits, where the call and WhatsApp buttons lock. It owns no colour, no face, no corner, no edge and no ornament. Everything that makes one site look like a specific restaurant is a **theme** in that client's `config.json`, generated by `factory/theme.mjs` from the restaurant's own Google photos, name, category and reviews (Claude vision), and repaired by contrast rules so any set stays readable. Two restaurants with different rooms get visibly different sites on the same skeleton; that is the product.

The hex values in the frontmatter are the restaurant vertical's **fallback** (`template/src/verticals/restaurant.json`), used only when theme generation fails. They are deliberately plain. They are not the system, and no design work should treat them as a reference look.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Theme tokens per client: `bg`, `ink`, `primary`, `accent`, `muted`, `display`, `body`, `displayWeight`, `radius`, `frame`, plus `weights` (the Google Fonts weights each family carries) and a `note` explaining the choice.
- Layout variants per client in `theme.layout`: `hero` photo | split | type, `menu` list | cards | board, `gallery` grid | strip | single | none, `rhythm` tight | airy, `order` menu-first | about-first. Every combination renders; a missing or unknown value falls back to the first of each list. All CSS and Astro conditionals, no script.
- Computed at build: `--on-primary`, `--on-accent`, `--on-muted` by WCAG contrast (ink or bg, whichever reads better), `color-scheme` from bg luminance, `--frame-w`/`--frame-c` from `frame`.
- One stylesheet, zero hardcoded hex or family in components; secondary text and rules are `currentColor` faded via `color-mix`, so they follow whatever surface they sit on.
- Structure: primary field with the name and a photo patch (hero), menu patches with a fixed price column (the largest group on primary), about with the rating, gallery in whole cells, hours patch, contact on the primary field with the map, locked call/WhatsApp bar below 800px.
- Motion is one authored moment: the hero words and photo rise into place on load (500ms exponential ease-out, `@starting-style`), 160ms hover and press. Reduced motion gated.
- Every element survives 0 photos (the name's initial stands in), 2 to 30 character names, 6 to 20 items, with or without a rating.

## Colors

Five theme roles per client and three computed on-colours. What each role does is fixed by the skeleton; what colour it is comes from the restaurant.

- **`--primary`**: the field behind the name at the top and behind the contact block at the bottom, the `theme-color` meta, the scrollbar thumb, and the largest menu group's patch. Text on it is `--on-primary`.
- **`--accent`**: the one colour that asks for a tap. The primary button fill, link underlines, text selection, the focus ring, `accent-color`, `caret-color`, the rating fill, and a hovered menu row's rule. Text on it is `--on-accent`.
- **`--muted`**: the card surface. Menu group patches, the hours list, the hero photo frame background, the map frame. Text on it is `--on-muted`.
- **`--bg`**: the page ground and the sticky bar. Drives `color-scheme`.
- **`--ink`**: body text on the ground and the solid frame when `frame: thick`.
- **Ink 2 / Ink 3 / Line** (`currentColor` at 72% / 44% / 18%): secondary text, the unfilled rating, hairlines.

### Named Rules
**The Role Not Hex Rule.** Components reference the five roles and the computed `--on-*` only. A client with any token set must stay readable without touching CSS.

**The Follow-The-Surface Rule.** Secondary text and rules are `currentColor` faded via `color-mix`, never a fixed neutral. On a patch the text colour is set once (`--on-muted` or `--on-primary`) and every faded tone inside follows it.

**The Repair Rule.** `fixTheme` in `factory/theme.mjs` enforces ink/bg at 7:1, a readable on-colour (4.5:1) for primary, accent and muted, accent at 3:1 against bg, a valid catalogue face and weight, and a visible edge when muted equals bg. `verify` warns when a config would be repaired.

## Typography

**Display:** `--font-display` at `--display-weight`, the one weight the theme chose from what the family carries. Headings never ask for a weight the face lacks, so no synthesized bold. **Body:** `--font-body` at 400 to 700. Both come from the catalogue in `factory/theme.mjs` (every face renders ë and ç; the catalogue skips the faces every generated site reaches for).

### Hierarchy
- **Display**: the restaurant name only, sized from its longest word via `--chars` (set in Hero) so it fills a 390px phone whatever the length; `overflow-wrap: anywhere` is the floor.
- **Headline**: section headings. 1rem below.
- **Title**: menu group names; the desktop brand link at 1rem.
- **Lede**: the hero headline under the name, max 34ch, in `--on-primary`. Section ledes are body size in Ink 2 at max 44ch.
- **Body**: paragraphs at max 60ch. Item names, times and prices at 600; the rating figure at 700.
- **Small**: item descriptions (max 30ch) and the footer line.

### Named Rules
**The Fixed Column Rule.** Every figure is tabular. Prices sit right-aligned in a `min-width: 6.5ch` column, `white-space: nowrap`; times never wrap.

**The Longest Word Rule.** The name is sized from its longest word, not its character count.

**The No Label Rule.** No uppercase label, kicker or eyebrow. Headline plus lede is the whole hierarchy.

## Layout

One column, one reading axis, one page. `.wrap` is `min(100% - 2.5rem, 70rem)`, centred. Sections carry `padding-block: var(--space-section)`; consecutive sections drop their top padding. The spacing scale is the `rhythm` variant, set as `data-rhythm` on `<body>`: **tight** is `--space-section: clamp(3rem, 7vw, 5.5rem)`, `--space-block: 2rem` (heading to content), `--space-heading: 1rem`; **airy** is `clamp(4.5rem, 10vw, 8.5rem)`, `3rem`, `1.5rem`.

The single breakpoint is 800px. Below: desktop nav hidden, hero CTAs hidden (the sticky bar carries them), body padded for the bar, menu and gallery stack. At 800px and up: the hero splits into words and photo, menu patches go two columns dense with the largest group spanning both, the gallery repacks into whole cells, contact and map sit side by side.

Order: Hero, then Items and About in the `order` variant's sequence (**menu-first**: Items, About, Testimonials; **about-first**: About, Testimonials, Items), then Gallery, Hours, Contact, StickyBar. Testimonials always follow About. Gallery, Testimonials and Hours render nothing when their data is empty; Gallery also renders nothing when the variant is `none`.

### Layout variants

- **Hero `photo`** (default): the words on the primary field, the photo as a square patch beside them on desktop and a 4:3 patch below them on a phone. No photo: the initial patch.
- **Hero `split`**: the photo bleeds to the viewport edge. On a phone it leads, edge to edge, capped at 48vh, the name under it. On desktop it fills the right half of the field, full height (min 32rem), the words and the hours fringe in the left half with the wrap's gutter. Without a photo it renders as `type`.
- **Hero `type`**: no photo. The name is sized to fill the wrap (`--chars` rule, cap 9.5rem), the headline a size up (max 28ch), the hours fringe in one row of three on desktop.
- **Menu `list`** (default): group patches with rows, the largest group on primary.
- **Menu `cards`**: every item its own muted patch, two across on a phone, `auto-fill` at 13rem on desktop; name, description, price at the bottom in 700. No group is highlighted.
- **Menu `board`**: one patch on primary holding every group; each line is name, a dotted leader in `currentColor` at 45%, price; descriptions small below the name; two CSS columns on desktop with groups unbroken.
- **Gallery `grid`** (default): whole cells, first photo large.
- **Gallery `strip`**: one flex row, `overflow-x: auto`, `scroll-snap-type: x mandatory`, gutters equal to the wrap's, each photo `min(78%, 30rem)` wide. The row is a focusable region; the page never scrolls sideways.
- **Gallery `single`**: the first photo alone, 4:3 on a phone, 21:9 capped at 36rem on desktop.
- **Gallery `none`**: no section.

## Elevation & Depth

No shadows. Depth is tonal: the primary field and the bg ground are two planes; patches sit on them in muted or primary. `frame` decides the edge: `none`, `line` (1px `currentColor` at 22%), or `thick` (3px `--ink`). The sticky bar separates from the page with a 1px line.

## Shapes

`--radius` is the theme's corner language (`0px`, `8px`, `20px` from the generator; any length by hand) and applies to every patch, photo, button and focus ring. The sticky bar is edge-to-edge and keeps no corner. The only drawn glyph is a 24px star tile, used as a mask for the rating so it takes any token colour.

## Components

### Buttons
Inline-flex, `min-height: 3.25rem`, `padding: 0.8rem 1.4rem`, weight 700, `border-radius: var(--radius)`.
- **Primary (`.btn.primary`):** fill and border `--accent`, text `--on-accent`. Once per surface: WhatsApp in the hero, contact and sticky bar.
- **Outline (`.btn`):** transparent, `1.5px solid currentColor`. "Menyja" in the hero, "Thirr" in the sticky bar.
- **Hover** (fine pointer): outline gets `currentColor` at 12%; primary mixes 12% of its text colour in; 160ms. **Press:** `scale(.97)`. **Focus:** 3px `--accent` outline.

### Patches (cards, photos, map)
`background: var(--patch, var(--muted))`, `border: var(--frame-w) solid var(--frame-c)`, `border-radius: var(--radius)`, `overflow: hidden`. The largest menu group sets `--patch: var(--primary)` and `color: var(--on-primary)`.

### Menu Row
Grid `1fr auto`, baseline aligned, `padding-block: .8rem .75rem`, a 1px hairline above in `currentColor` at 30% that turns `--accent` on hover (160ms).

### Hours List and Hero Fringe
`dt` day (capitalised) and `dd` time (600, nowrap) in a space-between flex row, divided by the same hairline. The fringe shows up to three compacted rows, or the first plus "Shih orarin e plotë".

### No-photo Hero
The photo patch shows the name's first character in the display face, in `--accent` on `--muted`, sized by container query. Never a grey box. (The `type` variant drops the patch altogether and lets the name carry the field.)

### Photo Credit
`figcaption.credit` as the last child of a photo `figure`, outside the patch: `.8rem`, `--ink-2`, flex with a `.9rem` gap, "Foto nga <author>" (linked to the contributor page when there is a URI) and "Powered by Google". Rendered for Google photos only; Instagram and owner photos and old string photos carry nothing. Same element in the hero, the gallery grid, the strip and the single photo.

### Filler Tag
`.tag` next to a menu item's name when `items[].source` is `filler`: `.72rem` at 600, `1px` border in `currentColor` at 40%, `--radius` corners, text in `--ink-2`, label from `labels.sample` ("shembull"). Follows the surface it sits on, so it reads the same on a muted card, the primary board and the primary list patch. Evidence items carry nothing.

### Testimonials
`ul.quotes` under `labels.testimonials`: `auto-fit` columns at 18rem, each quote a hairline-topped column (`currentColor` at 30%), the text at `1.15rem` / 1.45 with `max-width: 36ch`, the first name below at 600 in `--ink-2`. No cards, no quote glyphs, no stars.

### Menu Cards and Board
Cards: `.patch.card` on `--muted`, `padding: 1rem 1rem 1.1rem` (a touch more on desktop), grid gap `.3rem`, price last at 700. Board: `.patch.board` on `--primary` with `--on-primary` text, `padding: 1.5rem clamp(1.25rem, 4vw, 2.5rem) 1.75rem`, lines of `fit-content(100%) minmax(1.5rem, 1fr) auto` with a `2px dotted` leader sitting `.45em` above the baseline row.

### Sticky Bar
Phones only. Fixed bottom, `--bg` ground with a 1px top line, two equal columns: "Thirr" plain and WhatsApp in `--accent`, `min-height: 3.75rem`, no radius, a 1px line between them.

### Rise (signature motion)
`.rise` elements start at `opacity: 0; translate: 0 10px` via `@starting-style` and settle over 500ms with `cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)`; `.late` adds 120ms. Hero words and photo only.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** reference colours by role and put text on coloured surfaces in the matching computed `--on-*`.
- **Do** take every corner from `--radius` and every edge from `--frame-w`/`--frame-c`.
- **Do** use `--display-weight` for every heading; never ask a face for a weight it lacks.
- **Do** keep the 800px breakpoint as the only layout switch, and the sticky bar reachable at every scroll position below it.
- **Do** gate motion behind `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference` with the resting state already drawn.
- **Do** read all copy from `config.json` and `verticals/<vertical>.json` labels.
- **Do** read section shapes from `theme.layout` through `layout` in `lib/config.ts` and photos through `photoOf()`; a variant is a conditional and a stylesheet block, never a second component tree or a script.
- **Do** keep every combination of the five variants buildable; a variant that only works with a photo degrades to the one that works without.

### Don't:
- **Don't** add ornament, texture, pattern, or any device that makes the skeleton look like one particular place; the look is the client's theme, not the template's.
- **Don't** hardcode a hex, a font family, a radius or a border width in a component.
- **Don't** assume ink is light and bg is dark, or that primary is saturated; a theme may be quiet or loud.
- **Don't** add client JavaScript, shadows, blur or gradient fills.
- **Don't** add uppercase labels, kickers or eyebrows above headings.
- **Don't** hardcode Albanian strings in components or introduce a second display face.
